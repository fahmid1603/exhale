# Exhale 1.0.405

_Released October 10, 2026_

Ultra fluid. Knows your music. Liquid glass in every page, a player that flies out of the dock,
music videos over the song's own sound, and a search that finds a song from a link or a line of it.

### Added

**Music video in the player.** The artist's own video, picked over fan uploads, plays over the
song's own stream, with a full-screen landscape player in liquid glass: clear discs for ten
seconds back, play and ten seconds on, and a thin scrubber that thickens under your finger.

**Horizontal lyrics.** Turn the phone on its side from the lyrics' own buttons: the cover large
on the left, the lyrics lighting a word at a time on the right.

**Search that knows your music.**

- Paste a Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, Tidal, SoundCloud or YouTube link to get the same song or
  album here, with the ordinary results for it underneath.
- Type a line of a song to find it. Matches carry a **Lyrics match** tag and the line they matched.
- Import public Spotify playlists from Library or from a pasted link.

**Artist pages, rebuilt.** Apple Music's photograph and title logo for the artist, the latest
release on its own card, twenty top songs instead of five, and the story at the end of the page.

**Also new**

- 8D audio that passes behind your head, and one-tap Sound presets: Sped up, Nightcore, Slowed,
  Daycore and Vaporwave.
- Chromecast, from the device sheet.
- Offline mode, automatic with no internet.
- AMOLED black theme.
- "Play … on Exhale" from Gemini or Assistant.
- Word-by-word lyrics on the always-on display.
- Settings search lands on the setting itself and lights it.
- A jelly dock style, with labels, compact and touch-glow options.
- Version-specific changelogs: this page now shows the notes for the version you are running.

### Changed

- Real liquid glass in pages, not only the dock; the mini player is clear glass too.
- The dock opens on every page and folds as you scroll, the same on every page.
- The mini player is the same slim size on every tab.
- Library redesigned: a Liked card with Play, glass collection tiles, one destination list.
- The cover flies out of the dock into the player and home again.
- The player sheet moves like Apple Music's.
- Menus grow from where you touched.
- The Search bar setting moves the Search page's field, browsing and typing.
- The moving artwork runs at 30 frames a second, and only while music plays.
- Songs you have never played start in under a second.

### Fixed

- The queue was never actually saved, so it did not come back after Android closed the app.
- Resuming a song could crash.
- Songs sat at 00:00 or stopped in runs.
- Fan lyric videos were picked over the artist's own; full-screen video dropped the video and
  stayed sideways on the way out.
- Lyrics ran ahead, read a line twice, or stopped loading after one source failed.
- A pasted link said "No results found" under its own match.
- A black band across the top of inner pages.
- Lag opening the player from pages heavy with glass.
- Automix could stretch a song by up to 15%; it now stays within 6%.
